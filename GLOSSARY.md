# REVIVO

REVIVO is a wardrobe-first app that helps people understand, restyle and upcycle clothes they already own, instead of buying new ones.

## Scanning

**Scan**:
One analysis of one garment photo: the features the AI observed, its fabric guess and the knowledge-base matches. A Scan is an event, not something the user owns.
_Avoid_: Analysis, result, upload

**Correction**:
A user's fix to a Scan's fabric or garment type, or their yes/no confirmation of a suggested Named Style. It is kept as labelled data.
_Avoid_: Edit, feedback

**Concept Image**:
An AI-generated picture of what a garment could look like after a Recipe. It is always labelled as a concept, never presented as a real photo.
_Avoid_: After photo, render (in user-facing copy)

**Named Style**:
A specific, recognised design tradition or technique from a fixed list, such as breton-stripe, paisley or kimono-sleeve. It is distinct from generic descriptors like "stripes" or "cotton".
_Avoid_: Style tag, pattern (when the specific tradition is meant)

## Knowledge

**Knowledge Base**:
The team's curated, source-cited content that every factual statement in the app comes from.
_Avoid_: Database, fashion library, dataset

**Redesign Recipe**:
A reviewed, general way to transform a kind of garment, with a difficulty level, tools and outline steps. Everyone shares the same Recipes.
_Avoid_: Redesign idea, template, DIY suggestion

**Free Recipe**:
The single Redesign Recipe each Scan offers to every user: the best-matching beginner one where there is one.
_Avoid_: Starter recipe, sample

**History Entry**:
A reviewed, source-cited note on the cultural or historical roots of a Named Style. It applies only when that Named Style is recognised.
_Avoid_: Fun fact, story, insight

**Fabric Note**:
A reviewed fact or care note about a fabric.
_Avoid_: Fabric insight

**Guide**:
The personalised step-by-step instructions written for one Scan from one Redesign Recipe.
_Avoid_: Tutorial, recipe (when the personalised version is meant)

## Wardrobe

**Garment**:
A piece of clothing or an Accessory the user owns and has added to their Closet, by saving a Scan, by Quick Add, or by uploading an Accessory.
_Avoid_: Item, piece, closet item, product

**Closet**:
The user's collection of Garments, clothing and accessories alike.
_Avoid_: Wardrobe (that word is reserved for the Wardrobe OS), inventory

**Accessory**:
A Garment that isn't clothing, such as an earring, bag, belt or pair of shoes. It is used in Outfits but never scanned for history or Recipes.
_Avoid_: Extra, add-on

**Upload**:
Adding one Garment to the Closet. Free users have a limited number; Premium users have no limit.
_Avoid_: Scan (a Scan is an analysis, not an addition to the Closet)

**Quick Add**:
Adding a clothing Garment to the Closet for styling only, without the full upcycling Scan.
_Avoid_: Fast upload, lite scan

**Look**:
One Outfit the Wardrobe OS generates. Each "Style me" request returns three Looks.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, recommendation

**Allowance**:
The number of Uploads and "Style me" requests a free user has left. It starts with 10 of each and then refills gradually to a weekly amount.
_Avoid_: Quota, credits (in user-facing copy)

**Activity Record**:
The user's history of everything they uploaded, reconstructed and styled.
_Avoid_: Log, feed, timeline

**Upcycled Garment**:
A new Garment created when the user completes a Guide. It is linked to the Garment it was made from and to the Recipe used.
_Avoid_: Redesigned item, after

**Transformed Garment**:
A Garment that has been turned into an Upcycled Garment and so no longer exists in its original form.
_Avoid_: Retired, archived

**Style Profile**:
The user's stated tastes, colours to avoid, typical occasions and climate.
_Avoid_: Preferences, persona

**Outfit**:
A Look the user chose to keep: a combination of Garments they already own.
_Avoid_: Suggestion (for one that was kept)

## Product

**Wardrobe OS**:
The styling part of REVIVO: the Closet plus Looks generated from it. It is free within the Allowance.
_Avoid_: WOS (in user-facing copy), stylist

**Premium**:
The paid tier that gives unlimited Uploads and Looks and unlocks every Redesign Recipe beyond the Free Recipe.
_Avoid_: Pro, subscription (for the tier itself)

**Garment Story Card**:
A shareable image summarising one scanned piece of clothing: what it is, its fabric, its verified history and a chosen Recipe. It can be made before the piece is saved as a Garment.
_Avoid_: Share card, receipt

**Avatar**:
A stylised AI drawing of the user, made once from their full-length photo and used to show Looks on them. It is always labelled as a concept.
_Avoid_: Twin, model, mannequin
